## 摘要
超大规模多输入多输出（XL-MIMO）能够实现第六代（6G）通信中近场位置特定的波束聚焦。均匀圆形阵列（UCA）具有旋转对称性和均匀方位角覆盖，已成为近场XL-MIMO系统的关键使能架构。在本文中，我们提出了一种用于近场UCA系统的分辨率感知分层码本，以及一种高效的两阶段波束训练方案，以显著降低训练开销。具体而言，我们基于几何球面波传播模型量化了近场区域UCA系统的最小可分辨距离，揭示了其在角度-距离联合域中的空间分辨能力。在此结果的指导下，我们设计了针对UCA的分层码本，其中高能效的距离鲁棒波束成形（DRBF）码本提供粗略的方位角定位，而根据最小可分辨距离采样的全精度（FP）码本则实现精细的角度-距离波束搜索。正则化模态补偿抑制了弱模态放大，并在单位范数传输下的绝对幅度增益与距离鲁棒性之间提供了可控的权衡。基于该码本，我们开发了一种分层解耦架构贝叶斯回归（HDA-BAR）方案，用于快速、准确的近场波束训练。对于所考虑的阵列配置，所得到的HDA-BAR训练过程需要384个探测时隙，相对于传统的近场穷举搜索基准，训练开销减少了约99.66%。

## Abstract
Extremely large-scale multiple-input multiple-output (XL-MIMO) enables near-field location-specific beam focusing for sixth-generation (6G) communications. Uniform circular arrays (UCAs), with rotational symmetry and uniform azimuth coverage, have emerged as a key enabling architecture for near-field XL-MIMO systems. In this paper, we propose a resolution-aware hierarchical codebook for near-field UCA systems, along with an efficient two-stage beam training scheme to significantly reduce the training overhead. Specifically, we characterize the minimum resolvable distance of UCA systems in the near-field region based on a geometric spherical-wave propagation model, revealing their spatial resolution capability in the joint angle--distance domain. Guided by this result, we design a UCA-specific hierarchical codebook, where a power-efficient distance-robust beamforming (DRBF) codebook provides coarse azimuth localization and a full-precision (FP) codebook sampled according to the minimum resolvable distance enables refined angle--distance beam search. The regularized modal compensation suppresses weak-mode amplification and provides a controllable tradeoff between absolute amplitude gain under unit-norm transmission and distance robustness. Based on this codebook, we develop a hierarchical decoupled-architecture Bayesian regression (HDA-BAR) scheme for fast and accurate near-field beam training. For the considered array configuration, the resulting HDA-BAR training procedure requires $384$ probing slots, corresponding to an approximately \(99.66\%\) overhead reduction relative to the conventional near-field exhaustive-search benchmark.
