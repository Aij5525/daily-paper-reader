## 摘要
流体天线系统（FAS）通过从密集候选栅格中激活少数辐射端口来获得空间自由度，但决定激活哪些端口预设了对从未测量过的端口的信道知识。先前的端口选择工作假设完全或近乎完全的信道状态信息（CSI），并将导频开销视为固定成本。我们转而将端口选择和导频分配联合建模为主动推断。一个时空生成模型——亚波长空间相关性以及阶p自回归时间先验——维护对所有N个端口信道的高斯信念，发射机通过最小化期望自由能来选择其服务的端口以及在其中导频的子集，该自由能在可实现速率与信息增益和端口切换成本之间进行权衡。选择是贪婪且子模的，每时隙复杂度为O(NM)。在具有n_RF = 2K的混合前端、441端口栅格上，智能体仅测量2.3%的候选端口便达到全CSI理想接收机的和速率的91%；然后可以从仍然服务的端口撤回导频，撤回五分之一端口导频损失2%和速率，撤回五分之二损失8%。无需训练。

## Abstract
Fluid antenna systems (FAS) obtain spatial degrees of freedom by activating a few radiating ports from a dense grid of candidates, but deciding which ports to activate presupposes channel knowledge at ports that are never measured. Prior port-selection work assumes full or near-full channel state information (CSI) and treats the pilot overhead as a fixed cost. We instead formulate port selection and pilot allocation jointly as active inference. A spatio-temporal generative model -- sub-wavelength spatial correlation together with an order-p autoregressive temporal prior -- maintains a Gaussian belief over all N port channels, and the transmitter chooses both the ports it serves and the subset of them it pilots by minimizing an expected free energy that trades achievable rate against information gain and port-switching cost. Selection is greedy and submodular, at O(NM) per slot. On a 441-port grid with a hybrid front end at n_RF = 2K, the agent attains 91% of a full-CSI genie's sum rate while measuring only 2.3% of the candidate ports; pilots can then be withdrawn from ports that are still served, one fifth of them for 2% of the sum rate and two fifths for 8%. No training is required.
