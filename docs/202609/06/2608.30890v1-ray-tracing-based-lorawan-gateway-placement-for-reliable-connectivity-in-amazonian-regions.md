## 摘要
网络规划是无线通信中的一项重要任务，因为它帮助网络运营商避免不必要的成本。在物联网背景下，在亚马逊雨林中使用远距离广域网络技术，一个关键挑战是确保终端设备与网关（GW）之间的可靠通信。在这个意义上，这种可靠性受到信道条件的强烈影响。因此，在规划阶段，选择适当的信道模型是进行准确模拟的重要决策。基于这一动机，在这项工作中，我们提出了一个优化模型来评估不同类型信道对森林场景中覆盖率和数据包传递率的影响。我们使用了来自光线追踪、经验性和随机方法的信道，以评估在网络规划阶段作出的决策（就所使用的信道而言）如何影响网关部署，特别是终端设备的覆盖百分比和通信系统的可靠性。我们的结果表明，基于站点无关信道的网关部署可能高估满足网络要求所需的网关数量，而使用站点特定信道则可以用更少的网关满足相同的要求。

## Abstract
Network planning is an important task in wireless communications, as it helps network operators avoid unnecessary costs. In the context of the internet of things, using long-range wide-area network technologies in the Amazon rainforest, a key challenge is ensuring reliable communication between end-devices and gateways (GWs). In this sense, this reliability is strongly affected by channel conditions. Thus, during the planning phase, choosing the appropriate channel model is an important decision for accurate simulations. Given this motivation, in this work, we propose an optimization model to evaluate the impact of different types of channels on coverage and packet delivery ratio in a forest scenario. We used channels from ray tracing, empirical, and stochastic approaches to assess how decisions made during the network planning phase, in terms of the channel used, affect GW placement and, specifically, the percentage of end-devices covered and the reliability of the communication system. Our results show that GW placement based on site-independent channels can overestimate the number of GWs required to meet the network requirements, whereas using site-specific channels allows us to satisfy the same requirements with fewer GWs.
