## 摘要
与传统通信服务不同，6G集成感知与通信（ISAC）服务需要完整的端到端感知操作，从数据采集到处理、结果生成和暴露。在本文中，我们提出了SESO-ISAC，一种服务感知的端到端感知编排框架，它在感知执行上下文下将感知服务请求映射到完整的感知配置。SESO-ISAC不是独立地处理感知配置决策，而是捕获它们在端到端感知操作中的相互依赖性，根据适用的QoS要求评估候选配置，并在感知执行上下文变化时重新评估所选配置。通过两个代表性的案例研究，我们表明，优选的感知配置取决于适用QoS维度上的整体性能，并且可以在操作过程中改变。

## Abstract
Unlike conventional communication services, 6G Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) services require complete end-to-end sensing operations, from data collection to processing, result generation, and exposure. In this article, we propose SESO-ISAC, a service-aware end-to-end sensing orchestration framework that maps a sensing service request to a complete sensing configuration under the sensing execution context. Rather than treating sensing configuration decisions independently, SESO-ISAC captures their interdependence across the end-to-end sensing operation, evaluates candidate configurations according to the applicable QoS requirements, and re- evaluates the selected configuration as the sensing execution context changes. Through two representative case studies, we show that the preferred sensing configuration depends on the overall performance across the applicable QoS dimensions and can change during operation.
