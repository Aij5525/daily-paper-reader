## 摘要
在每次无线地图更新时探测所有候选位置开销很大。本文提出了电磁孪生（ET）波束赋形，将稀疏空间探针转化为和速率决策。ET存储依赖于位置的角功率谱，通过图正则化估计更新其创新信息，并查询用户协方差以进行投影波束优化。通过查询加权的后验方差缩减，速率敏感度界选择后续探针。在1%随机探针下，ET波束赋形达到2.69 bit/s/Hz，而静态信道知识图为1.12 bit/s/Hz；在7%查询感知探针下，达到3.03 bit/s/Hz，与完美协方差波束赋形相差3.4%以内。

## Abstract
Probing every candidate location at each wireless-map update is costly. This letter develops electromagnetic-twin (ET) beamforming that converts sparse spatial probes into a sum-rate decision. The ET stores a location-dependent angular power spectrum, updates its innovation through graph-regularized estimation, and queries user covariances for projected beam optimization. A rate-sensitivity bound selects subsequent probes by query-weighted posterior-variance reduction. With 1\% random probes, ET beamforming achieves $2.69$ bit/s/Hz versus $1.12$ for a static channel knowledge map; with 7\% query-aware probes, it reaches $3.03$ bit/s/Hz, within 3.4\% of perfect-covariance beamforming.
