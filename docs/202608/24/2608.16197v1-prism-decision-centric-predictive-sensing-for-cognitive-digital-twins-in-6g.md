## 摘要
集成感知与通信（ISAC）和数字孪生（DT）技术已成为未来无线网络的互补技术，这些网络需要涉及物理世界与数字世界持续交互的自主操作。然而，现有的DT辅助ISAC框架在连续、无差别感知的同时仅优化单一任务，几乎没有为持久的多领域知识或主动感知控制留下空间。本文提出了一种预测性、推理驱动的智能感知模块（PRISM）引擎，将DT从被动的、特定领域的优化器转变为持久的、全网范围的推理系统。PRISM支持决策中心的预测感知，主动将感知引导至预期的决策需求，而非遵循固定的感知计划。通过一个具有混合eMBB、URLLC和mMTC设备群体的示意性超大规模多输入多输出（XL-MIMO）部署场景，我们展示了这一原则如何有益于用于信道获取的可见区域感知，并支持切片感知操作。初步仿真（包括该部署场景以及由此产生的知识误差、开销和延迟结果）证实，这种决策中心方法显著降低了感知开销，同时保持了决策可靠性和延迟，支持所提出的架构作为迈向自感知、自主编排的6G网络的实际步骤。

## Abstract
Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) and Digital Twin (DT) technology have emerged as complementary for future wireless networks that require autonomous operations involving continuous interaction between physical and digital worlds. However, existing DT-assisted ISAC frameworks sense continuously and indiscriminately while optimizing only a single task, leaving little room for persistent, multi-domain knowledge or proactive sensing control. This article proposes a Predictive, Reasoning-driven, Intelligent Sensing Module (PRISM) engine that transforms the DT from a passive, domain-specific optimizer into a persistent, network-wide reasoning system. PRISM enables decision-centric predictive perception, proactively directing sensing toward anticipated decisions needs rather than following fixed sensing schedules. Using an illustrative extremely large multiple-input multiple-output (XL-MIMO) deployment scenario with a mixed eMBB, URLLC, and mMTC device population, we show how this principle benefits visibility-region sensing for channel acquisition and supports slice-aware operation. Preliminary simulations, including this deployment scenario and the resulting knowledge error, overhead, and latency results, confirm that this decision-centric approach substantially reduces sensing overhead while preserving decision reliability and latency, supporting the proposed architecture as a practical step toward self-aware, autonomously orchestrated 6G networks.
