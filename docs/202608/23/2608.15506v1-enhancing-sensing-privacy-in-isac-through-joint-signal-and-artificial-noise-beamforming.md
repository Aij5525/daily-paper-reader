## 摘要
集成感知与通信（ISAC）是6G网络中的一个有前景的特性。它被设想为能够提高频谱效率，并提供满足未来应用严格要求的感知和通信服务。然而，它也给恶意攻击者提供了关于网络的新信息，从而带来了新的安全和隐私问题。在这项工作中，我们通过研究一个位置未知的感知窃听者（EVE）的能力，其充当无源双基地雷达（PBR）以获取用户位置信息，来关注单站ISAC系统的感知隐私。然后，我们提出一个联合发射和人工噪声（AN）波束成形优化问题，以降低EVE的性能。最后，我们提出一种迭代算法来求解所提出的优化问题并评估其性能。

## Abstract
Integrated sensing and communications (ISAC) is a promising feature in 6G networks. It is envisioned to enhance spectral efficiency and provide sensing and communication services that meet the stringent requirements of future applications. However, it also poses new security and privacy concerns by giving malicious attackers access to new information about the network. In this work, we focus on the sensing privacy of a monostatic ISAC system by investigating the capability of a sensing eavesdropper (EVE) with an unknown location, acting as a passive bistatic radar (PBR) to gain access to user location information. We then propose a joint transmit and artificial noise (AN) beamforming optimization problem to degrade EVE's performance. Finally, we propose an iterative algorithm to solve the proposed optimization problem and evaluate its performance.
