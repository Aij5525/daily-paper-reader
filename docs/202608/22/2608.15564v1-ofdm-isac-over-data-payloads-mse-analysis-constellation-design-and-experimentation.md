## 摘要
正交频分复用（OFDM）凭借其高频谱效率以及与现有无线标准的天然兼容性，已成为集成感知与通信（ISAC）系统的关键波形。然而，在随机数据调制下其基本的估计理论感知性能仍未得到充分探索。本文针对OFDM-based ISAC系统在多目标距离估计中的性能提出了一种统一且显式的分析方法，重点研究了调制星座对感知性能的不同影响。我们建立了一个全面的估计理论框架，用以刻画匹配滤波（MF）和互易滤波（RF）两类感知接收机架构下的距离估计均方误差（MSE）。理论分析表明，在多目标及杂波密集环境中，MF接收机的感知性能根本上受到星座四阶矩（峰度）的限制，该参数决定了数据依赖的旁瓣干扰水平。相比之下，RF接收机以噪声增强为代价消除了此类干扰，其性能由星座的二阶逆矩决定。基于这些闭式MSE推导，我们提出了一种面向感知接收机的几何星座整形（GCS）框架。通过联合优化基于最小欧氏距离（MED）的星座几何结构以及接收机相关的感知指标，我们实现了通信可靠性与感知精度之间的灵活权衡。结果表明，所提出的星座整形在实际空中传输实现中能够显著提升性能，并促进不同接收机架构下感知与通信的定制化权衡。

## Abstract
Orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (OFDM) is a key waveform for integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) systems due to its high spectral efficiency and inherent compatibility with modern wireless standards. However, its fundamental estimation-theoretic sensing performance under random data modulation remains largely unexplored. This paper presents a unified and explicit performance analysis of OFDM-based ISAC systems for multi-target range estimation, focusing on the distinct impacts of the modulation constellation on the sensing performance. We develop a comprehensive estimation-theoretic framework to characterize the range estimation mean-square error (MSE) for both matched filtering (MF) and reciprocal filtering (RF) sensing receiver architectures. Our theoretical analysis reveals that in multi-target and clutter-rich environments, the sensing performance of the MF receiver is fundamentally limited by the fourth-order moment (kurtosis) of the constellation, which determines the data-dependent sidelobe interference level. In contrast, the RF receiver eliminates such interference at the cost of noise enhancement, with its performance governed by the inverse second-order moment of the constellation. Building on these closed-form MSE derivations, we propose a sensing-receiver specific geometric constellation shaping (GCS) framework. By jointly optimizing the constellation geometry based on the minimum Euclidean distance (MED) and receiver-dependent sensing metrics, we enable a flexible trade-off between communication reliability and sensing precision. Our results demonstrate that the proposed constellation shaping provides significant performance gains and facilitates a tailored sensing and communication trade-off across different receiver architectures in practical over-the-air implementations.
