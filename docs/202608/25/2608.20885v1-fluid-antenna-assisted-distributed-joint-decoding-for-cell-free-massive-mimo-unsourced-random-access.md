## 摘要
无源随机接入（URA）支持大规模突发连接，但仍受限于多用户干扰、分布式信道不确定性和局部衰落。本文提出了一种用于无小区大规模MIMO URA的流体天线辅助分布式联合解码器。单射频链路接入点顺序探测相关端口；中央处理器随后执行相关性感知的期望最大化近似消息传递，为每个接入点选择一个公共净荷端口，并将MIMO迭代高斯近似与全数据包信道重估计和连续干扰消除相结合。固定端口消融实验证实了分布式接收、重估计和迭代消除的互补优势，而流体天线仿真显示，与固定端口接收相比，平均接收功率和下尾接收功率均有所提高。该框架提供了流体天线与无小区URA的可行集成，并激励了在导频碰撞和切换开销下的端到端评估。

## Abstract
Unsourced random access (URA) supports massive sporadic connectivity but remains limited by multiuser interference, distributed channel uncertainty, and local fading. This paper proposes a fluid-antenna-assisted distributed joint decoder for cell-free massive MIMO URA. Single-radio-frequencychain access points sequentially sound correlated ports; the central processor then performs correlation-aware expectationmaximization approximate message passing, selects one common payload port per access point, and combines MIMO iterative Gaussian approximation with full-packet channel re-estimation and successive interference cancellation. Fixed-port ablations confirm the complementary benefits of distributed reception, reestimation, and iterative cancellation, while fluid-antenna simulations show improved average and lower-tail received power over fixed-port reception. The framework provides a feasible integration of fluid antennas and cell-free URA and motivates end-to-end evaluation under pilot collisions and switching overhead.
