## 摘要
我们考虑下行无线网络中信道统计未知的无线调度问题，其中基站向多个用户发送数据。调度性能在很大程度上依赖于准确的信道状态信息(CSI)，而获取CSI通常代价高昂。本文中，CSI仅在每次调度传输后从ACK/NACK反馈中获得。由于无线信道资源有限，所有用户不能同时被调度传输。因此，最近观察到的CSI可能已过时。传统上利用过时CSI解决调度问题的方法是使用信念状态，信念状态通过信道的时间相关统计来计算。然而，信道统计通常是未知的；因此，信念状态可能不可数，这种方法变得不可行。在本文中，我们为无线调度问题引入了一种新的充分统计量。为此，我们通过信道状态信息年龄(AoCSI)来表征CSI的过时程度，并表明最新观察到的CSI及其AoCSI是做出调度决策所需历史的充分统计量。因此，我们能够缩减在线学习的状态空间。我们的目标是开发一种在线调度算法，在满足信道资源约束的同时，最大化有限时间范围内所有用户的期望总吞吐量。所构建的问题是一个 restless 多臂老虎机(RMAB)问题。我们开发了一种在线最大增益优先(Online-MGF)策略，该策略在情节数上实现了次线性遗憾。对于ON/OFF信道的特例，我们能够证明可索引性，并推导出Whittle指数的闭式表达式。数值结果表明，Online-MGF策略在很少的情节数内即可收敛到具有已知统计的MGF和Whittle指数策略。

## Abstract
We consider a wireless scheduling problem in downlink wireless networks with unknown channel statistics, where a Base Station (BS) sends data to multiple users. The scheduling performance relies heavily on accurate Channel State Information (CSI), which is often costly to acquire. In this paper, CSI is obtained from ACK/NACK feedback, only after each scheduled transmission. Due to limited wireless channel resources, all users cannot be scheduled for transmission simultaneously. Hence, the most recently observed CSI can be outdated. The traditional approach to solve scheduling problems using outdated CSI is to utilize belief states, which are calculated using the time correlation statistics of channels. However, channel statistics are often unknown; consequently, belief states can be uncountable and this approach becomes infeasible. In this paper, we introduce a new sufficient statistics for the wireless scheduling problem. Towards this effort, we characterize the CSI staleness by the Age of Channel State Information (AoCSI) and show that the latest observed CSI and its AoCSI is a sufficient statistic of the history to make the scheduling decisions. Accordingly, we are able to reduce the state space for online learning. Our goal is to develop an online scheduling algorithm that maximizes the expected sum throughput of all users over a finite time-horizon while satisfying a channel resource constraint. The formulated problem is a Restless Multi-armed Bandit (RMAB). We develop an online Maximum Gain First (Online-MGF) policy, which achieves sub-linear regret on the number of episodes. For a special case of ON/OFF channels, we are able to prove indexability and derive a closed-form expression of the Whittle index. Numerical results demonstrate that the Online-MGF policy converges to MGF and Whittle index policies with known statistics within a very few episodes.
