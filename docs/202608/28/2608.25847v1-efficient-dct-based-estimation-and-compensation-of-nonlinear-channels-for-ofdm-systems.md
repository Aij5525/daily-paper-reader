## 摘要
本文提出了一种用于正交频分复用（OFDM）通信系统中非线性频率选择性信道估计的最大似然（ML）框架。非线性失真采用基于离散余弦变换（DCT）的表示进行建模，从而形成具有良好收敛性的良态估计问题。所提出的方法将紧凑参数化与低计算复杂度相结合，支持快速自适应和高效的实时实现。数值结果表明，所提出的信道估计可用于多种非线性补偿方法，并在极有限的训练开销下实现接近理想的误码率（BER）性能。在同时存在幅度和相位非线性时，该性能可保持至约15 dB的信噪比（SNR）；当仅存在幅度失真时，可保持至-10 dB。

## Abstract
This paper proposes a maximum-likelihood (ML) framework for estimating nonlinear frequency-selective channels in orthogonal frequency-division multiplexing (OFDM) communication systems. The nonlinear distortions are modeled using a Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT)-based representation, which results in a well-conditioned estimation problem with favorable convergence properties. The proposed method combines a compact parameterization with low computational complexity, enabling fast adaptation and efficient real-time implementation. Numerical results show that the proposed channel estimation can be used for multiple nonlinear compensation methods and achieve near-ideal BER performance with very limited training overhead. This performance is maintained down to approximately 15 dB SNR in the presence of both amplitude and phase nonlinearities, and down to -10 dB when only amplitude distortions are present.
