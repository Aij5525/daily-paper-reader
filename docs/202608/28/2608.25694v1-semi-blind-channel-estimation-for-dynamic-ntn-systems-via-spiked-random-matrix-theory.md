## 摘要
半盲信道估计在大规模无线系统中提供了导频开销与估计精度之间具有吸引力的权衡。然而，在高度动态的环境中，如非地面网络（NTN），信道获取变得尤为具有挑战性，因为快速变化的信道和高系统维度会因采样噪声而显著降低传统基于协方差估计器的性能。本文针对NTN系统中多用户上行链路提出了一种鲁棒的半盲信道估计框架。所提方法引入了一种最优正则化最小二乘公式，以平衡基于训练的信息与盲子空间结构。通过利用随机矩阵理论（RMT）框架中的尖峰协方差模型，我们推导了所得信道均方误差的闭式表征，并获得了最优正则化参数的解析可处理设计。所得估计器计算高效，特别适用于高维场景。在现实第三代合作伙伴计划（3GPP）NTN信道模型下的仿真结果表明，与传统的半盲和基于训练的估计器相比，所提方法具有显著的性能提升。

## Abstract
Semi-blind channel estimation offers an attractive tradeoff between pilot overhead and estimation accuracy in large-scale wireless systems. However, reliable channel acquisition becomes particularly challenging in highly dynamic environments such as non-terrestrial networks (NTNs), where rapidly varying channels and high system dimensionality significantly degrade the performance of conventional covariance-based estimators due to sampling noise. In this paper, we propose a robust semi-blind channel estimation framework for multi-user uplink systems operating in NTN systems. The proposed approach introduces an optimally regularized least-squares formulation that balances training-based information and blind subspace structure. By exploiting the spiked covariance model within a random matrix theory (RMT) framework, we derive a closed-form characterization of the resulting channel mean-squared error and obtain an analytically tractable design of the optimal regularization parameter. The resulting estimator is computationally efficient and particularly well suited to high-dimensional regimes. Simulation results under realistic Third Generation Partnership Project (3GPP) NTN channel models demonstrate substantial performance improvements over conventional semi-blind and training-based estimators.
